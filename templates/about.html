Our Story

20+ Years of Excellence

Satya Sai Baba Auto Electrical Works was founded in 2001 with a simple vision: to provide honest, expert auto electrical services to the people of Gudivada, Krishna District, Andhra Pradesh. What started as a small roadside workshop has grown into a fully equipped, Bosch Authorized service center.

We specialize in all aspects of automotive electrical systems — from starter motors and alternators to complete vehicle rewiring and modern diagnostics. Our team of certified technicians brings decades of combined experience to every job.
